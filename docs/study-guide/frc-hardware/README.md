# FRC: hardware and sensors

Motors, the sensors that tell the robot where things are, and the controller in the driver's hands.

- [TalonFX motor control and configuration](01-talonfx.md): the CAN bus, control requests, configurations, gear ratios, the motor's own loop, soft and current limits, and reading the motor back.
- [Sensors: gyro, encoders, limits](02-sensors.md): relative and absolute encoders, the CANcoder and the gyro, homing, wiring switches and beams to fail safe, debouncing, and whether a reading is real.
- [Joystick controls](03-joystick-controls.md): sticks and their signs, deadband, buttons and triggers, field-relative driving from either end, and the keyboard in SimGUI.
